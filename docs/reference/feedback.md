# How to give feedback

Sygnal is developed in the open, and feedback shapes it. This page explains where to send it.

## Something in the docs is unclear or wrong

[Open an issue](https://github.com/Medtronic-LABS/sygnal/issues/new?template=docs-unclear.md) and link to the page. Say what confused you; a suggested wording helps but isn't needed.

If you're comfortable with GitHub, you can also suggest the fix directly as a pull request. Every page lives in the repository's [`docs/`](https://github.com/Medtronic-LABS/sygnal/tree/main/docs) folder.

## A question about the specification

[Ask a question](https://github.com/Medtronic-LABS/sygnal/issues/new?template=spec-question.md). Tell us which API, schema or field it's about, and what you're building. Answers that would help others go back into the documentation.

## A change to the specification

[Propose a change](https://github.com/Medtronic-LABS/sygnal/issues/new?template=change-proposal.md). Describe the problem first, ideally with a real use case, then the change you suggest.

Proposals are discussed in the open before they are accepted. Accepted changes are recorded in the [changelog](../specification/changelog.md).

## Open design questions

Some parts of Sygnal are still being designed. They are listed on the [Open questions](open-questions.md) page. Views on any of them are welcome: open an issue and name the question in its title.

## Partnerships, pilots and the sandbox

* To discuss a pilot, a partnership or joining the [Sygnal Sandbox](../build/sandbox.md), email [hello@sygnal.network](mailto:hello@sygnal.network).
* For help once your platform is connected to the sandbox, email [support@sygnal.network](mailto:support@sygnal.network).

## What happens to your feedback

* Issues are read and labelled, and we aim to reply to each one.
* Fixes to the docs are made as they come in.
* Changes to the specification follow the steps above and are listed in the changelog, so you can see what changed and why.

See also [Contributing](contributing.md) for how to make changes yourself.
